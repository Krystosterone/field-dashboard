# Field

A dashboard for Home Assistant drawn as a hardware panel: keys you press, faders you slide and always-dark screens you
read. Every supported device in a room-by-room page, laid out for you and kept up to date, on a phone, a tablet or a
wall panel.

![Field on a tablet](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Krystosterone/dashhaus/main/docs/src/assets/skins/tablet-field-light.webp)

Each module has a key, a fader and a screen, with printed labels in capitals. A small orange mark shows what a
control sets. Field comes in two finishes, light grey Alloy and Black, and opens more in a panel from the module's
name.

## Install

You need Home Assistant 2026.5 or newer and an administrator account. Nothing to install on your computer.

1. [Open Field in your Home Assistant](https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/hacs_repository/?owner=Krystosterone&repository=field-dashboard&category=plugin)
   and press **Download**. Home Assistant 2026.11 or newer opens its Marketplace; an older one opens HACS. If you're
   asked to add a custom repository, say yes; its type is **Dashboard**.
2. Reload the browser tab.
3. **Settings → Dashboards → Add dashboard**, pick **Field** under Community dashboards, and press **Create**.

Field opens from the sidebar, laid out from your house. It follows Home Assistant's light or dark mode, and Field as
it updates. No other dashboard is touched.

More, with a wall panel's settings and your own layout: [krystosterone.github.io/dashhaus/field](https://krystosterone.github.io/dashhaus/field/).
